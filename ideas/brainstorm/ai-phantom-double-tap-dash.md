# Phantom double-tap dash in the AI

The scripted AI can emit a dash command that the sim reads as a double tap while the hero is pinned at a wall. WALL-3b skips wallHold when the command already carries dash, so the case is left alone. Find out whether the phantom dash costs the AI anything at the wall and whether to suppress it.
