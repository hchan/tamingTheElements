# QA r24: Escape does not pause or open a menu in a Versus fight

In a 3v3 tag Versus fight, pressing Escape changed nothing: no pause overlay, no Resume or Quit buttons, and the fight kept running (Sindarosa HP went 140 to 127 while "paused"; tg-06-pause, tg-07-resume). Tab then lands on the fullscreen button. Street Fighter and Marvel vs Capcom both offer a pause with Resume, Move list and Quit. Add an Escape or Start pause menu (Resume, Move list, Settings, Quit to menu) that freezes the sim tick.
