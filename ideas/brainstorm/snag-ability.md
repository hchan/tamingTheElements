# Snag (replaces Capture)

Rope root, an ability for only some heroes: Treenoc (Entangling Vines), Wispera (Sonar Blast), Curomo (Shards of Ice), Echo (Fade). Range ~3 tiles, cooldown 8 s, root 1.2 s (can turn, aim, cast; not move). Burned by fire projectiles, fire cone and Sun weather. Guard blocks it; Dash does not break it. No Capture wording in the UI. Arcade only. Owner approved 2026-11-11.
