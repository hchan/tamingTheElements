# Tour stage 1 foe Wispera reads as a small orb (r127)

`.scratch/r127/53-tour-fight.png`: Sindarosa is a large dragon, Wispera a roughly 100 px glowing orb with thin tendrils and a faint dark halo. Against a Street Fighter style roster, a foe at a fraction of the hero's size and with no limbs reads weak and gives no pose to react to on hits. Check Wispera's baked scale against the other heroes and consider a larger sprite or a stronger silhouette; the Tour stage 1 opener is the first fight a new player sees.
