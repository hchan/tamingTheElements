# QA r24: Settings page is 2500 px tall, debug options exposed, controls unstyled

Settings is about 2460-2510 px tall with 37 controls. Save and Back sit in the middle, above Audio, Record playtests, Unlock full garrison (tester mode), Cheats, Export and Import. Players should not see Cheats, the tester unlock or Record playtests (move them behind a hidden dev toggle). Audio sliders are default system blue and the TURN inputs are plain boxes, which breaks the mystic look.

Suggest: collapsible sections (Controls, Audio, Accessibility, Data), a sticky Save and Back bar at the bottom, themed sliders. Help (about 4200-4900 px) and Guide (about 3900 px) are also very long at 844x390; add a contents list or section jump links.
