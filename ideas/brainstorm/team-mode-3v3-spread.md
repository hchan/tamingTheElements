# 3v3 win-rate spread 10.6% to 85.2%

3v3 Arcade (24 seeds, Normal): fiara and wispera 85.2%, pax 81.4% versus lavax 12.5%, cyclonius 14.0%, sindarosa 10.6%; 2v2 spans 24.6% to 69.7%.
Partners are fixed per race in the harness, so this is a roster effect. Decide whether 3v3 balance matters; if so, re-fit with random or
player-chosen partners (see [team-harness-random-partners](team-harness-random-partners.md)) before touching stats. Rounds also hit the clock
1.1 times a match in 3v3 (0.08 in 2v2): consider a longer round clock or a smaller bench heal.

Source: [snag-trap-and-team-modes-findings.md](snag-trap-and-team-modes-findings.md).

Re-measured with random partners (section 3 of the findings): 49 pp (lavax 22.0% to wispera 71.3%), driven by two outlier races; see [wispera-team-dominance](wispera-team-dominance.md) and [lavax-team-weakness](lavax-team-weakness.md). The fixed-partner 10.6 to 85.2% was mostly roster luck.
