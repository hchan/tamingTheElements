# QA r10: menu, Versus, Settings, Help

- Menu: clean, five entries, version stamp. A "Dismiss" button exists in the DOM at load; confirm it is not tabbable when invisible.
- Versus picker: the "You" card says Sindarosa but "0 of 1 picked" while the detail pane offers "Add to team" for the same hero. A preselected hero should count as picked, or the card should say "Pick your fighter". Earth row is cut under the footer until scrolled.
- Picking a hero of any element and pressing Fight! works, but the stage always starts as fire lava and only changes via Weather. New players will not know the arena follows Weather; say so in the Weather tooltip or announce the stage change.
- Settings: styled with legends, good. Background ends about 580 px down, leaving a flat seam on tall pages.
- Help: wording clean, key list matches the action bar.
- First-run hints use number keys (1, 7, 8, 9); an SF player expects a punch/kick style preset. Offer a layout preset in Key Bindings.
