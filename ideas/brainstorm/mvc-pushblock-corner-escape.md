# MvC pushblock / corner escape

A cornered, guarding player has no escape beyond a dash. Consider a Marvel-style pushblock (a guard-time shove that opens space) or a reversal that swaps sides. Needs a design pass: it interacts with Guard, Dash and the corner pushback of WALL-2.

WALL-3 note: the Hard-only wallHold gives no corner escape; see hard-corner-release.md.
