# Summon temporary minions

User idea 2026-11-12: an ability that summons temporary minions (small allies that fight for a few seconds, then vanish). Design questions: count cap per hero, life span in ticks, minion hp and damage, whether they can be Snagged or hit by area attacks, AI targeting (distract the foe or attack), a magic or cooldown cost, upkeep drain as in Archon II (see archon-adept-summons and bomb-traps for the shared entity and timer plumbing). Sim change: new entity kind with an owner and expiry tick, all Int32Array state; use the add-ability skill, design first. Art: small baked sprites in the Fiara look.
