# Water and air hazard strike frames are hard to tell from the warning (r19)

From the stage-hazards shots in ~/claudetmp/qa-r19/haz/: on the water stage the strike shows as faint dark squares on blue, and on air the warning is thin grey streaks on pale ice. Fire is clear (orange cracks). Idea: give every element a strike flash (full-colour fill pulse plus a brief screen-edge tint) so the moment of danger reads at a glance, like an SF stage hazard.
