# Ranged primary spam: 7 heroes cast one shot for 85-99% of casts

`pnpm ability:usage` flags Cyclonius wind-cone, Fiara fireball, Ishimi slime-blast, Lavax crimson-fire, Morblass icicles, Rokcore rock-pellet and Sindarosa fire-blast as overused: 25 to 58 casts a minute, 0.4 to 0.9 hits per cast, and the second slot barely used. Decide per hero whether the second ability deserves a stronger role (cooldown, damage, AI trigger) or the shooter a longer cooldown. Needs design approval for data changes; measure with ability:usage and balance:compare.
