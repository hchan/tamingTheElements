# Research free sprites for inspiration

Owner idea 2026-11-12: research free sprite and art packs and get inspired by them (style, detail level, palettes, effect sprites), starting with what makes Pax, Curomo's ice-shock and other heroes look too simple.

Plan:
1. Survey free sources with clear licences (OpenGameArt, itch.io free asset packs, Kenney, LPC, CC0 sets); note licence terms for each.
2. Study, do not copy: record palette, outline weight, shading steps, silhouette, animation frame counts and effect sprite style in a short doc (`docs/ART-REFERENCES.md` or notes in this file), with links.
3. Only import an asset if its licence allows it (CC0 or attribution given in the licence notes) and it is listed in the asset pipeline docs; otherwise use it as reference only.
4. Apply findings to the rig generators in `tools/assets/rig-gen/` and the art-consistency batches (see hero-rigs, art-consistency-batches, ability-audit).
