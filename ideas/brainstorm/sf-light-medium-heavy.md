# Light / medium / heavy attacks

Source of the idea: Street Fighter II / Marvel vs Capcom. SFII's six buttons trade strength for speed. Give each hero a quick weak poke, a normal hit and a slow heavy (hold or second button), with different startup, damage, hitstun and cooldown. Needs a design section in FUN-DESIGN before sim work.
