# QA r24: most hero abilities have no distinctive hit effect

Across all 12 heroes (k-*-a1/a2/snag shots) abilityFx reports auras only for fireDance (Lavax), adrenaline (Pax) and rockArmour (Rokcore), plus HEAL (Fiara). The specials of Treenoc, Wispera, Ishimi, Morblass, Curomo and Cyclonius show only small rings and floating damage numbers. Snag (rope with ROOTED and SNAG pop) and the super banners (PHANTOM SQUALL, TITAN SLAM, CRIMSON TIDE, HAILSTORM, MOUNTAIN BREAKER, SIREN GALE) read well, so the gap is the everyday specials.

Street Fighter and Marvel vs Capcom give every special a flash, hit-spark and impact freeze. Give each special an element-coloured impact burst, hit-spark, short hit-stop and a named pop like the Snag one.
