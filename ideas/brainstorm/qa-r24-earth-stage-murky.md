# QA r24: earth stages look murky and olive, not bright or luminous

Earth arenas read dark olive and brown, while air and water stages are good. Add warm golden light shafts, brighter moss and crystal accents, and lift the midtones. Also, some fighters read as dark blue or black silhouettes mid-fight (possibly a status tint); verify, and give statuses an outline instead of a full darken.
