# Team-mode harness: randomise partners

`runMatch` takes partners at fixed list offsets (+6 for 2v2; +4 and +8 for 3v3), so a race's win rate is its roster's. Add a mode that draws
partners from the seed (excluding the foe's races), aggregate per race over many rosters, and report a per-race effect plus a pair-synergy
table. Tools only, no sim change.

Source: [snag-trap-and-team-modes-findings.md](snag-trap-and-team-modes-findings.md).
