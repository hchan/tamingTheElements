# Demo arena (?hero=&foe=) is dark with a debug-looking HUD

The flag fights (all 12 heroes, e.g. `?hero=fiara&foe=rokcore`) use the old demo layout: a near-black stage with faint specks, left/right text blocks ("HP 100% OK", "cap 100%", "rested") and a 3-slot bar (Attack/Special/Tag). It is nowhere near the bright luminous painterly stages of Versus. Fine as a dev tool, but it is the first thing a tester sees and gives a false impression of the art.

Idea: route the flag into the real Versus arena (hero-element stage, normal HUD) so hero QA judges real visuals. Screens: h-*-a.png, h-*-b.png.
