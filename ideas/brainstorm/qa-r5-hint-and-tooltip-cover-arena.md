# Fight-start hint card and stuck action tooltip cover the arena

The First Blood hint (top-left, about 330x220 px at 1280x720) sits over the player's side, the SUN/RAIN status chips and the first trap pad. On the phone it is about 210x100 CSS px of a 390 px tall screen and hides the left of the arena. A slot tooltip (Dash, or Weather in 2v2) is also shown bottom-centre with no hover in every shot, over the floor.

Proposal: auto-dismiss the hint on first move or after about 6 s, collapse it to one line on phones, and show tooltips only on real hover or focus-visible.
