# Diminishing returns on stuns and roots

Source of the idea: World of Warcraft arena PvP. WoW's CC rule: the same effect on a target halves its length, then immunity, with a reset window (about 16 to 20 s in recent patches, per the Warcraft Wiki). The window starts when the effect ends, and DR belongs to the target. Apply it to Snag, freeze and stun so chain-CC cannot lock a hero out. Needs a visible DR icon (not colour alone). Sim change: per-entity counters in an Int32Array.
