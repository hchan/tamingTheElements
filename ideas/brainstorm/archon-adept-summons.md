# Summon heroes with upkeep

Source of the idea: Archon II. Archon's adepts summon elementals and demons with a per-turn upkeep. In the arena: a hero spends magic to summon a short-lived ally or turret (fits the bomb and trap idea) that costs upkeep and vanishes when magic runs out.
