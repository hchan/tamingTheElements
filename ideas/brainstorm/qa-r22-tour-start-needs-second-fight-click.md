# Tour: Start tour opens stage select, then FIGHT is a second step

`#play` then Start tour shows STAGE 1 OF 9 with Fight and Main Menu (t-02-tour1.png). A player or tester who expects the tour to start waits on a menu that looks like a loading screen. After FIGHT is pressed the 3v3 tag fight works well: hint card, HUD, bench portraits (t-10-after-fight.png).

Idea: focus the Fight button by default and pulse it, or label it Start stage 1. Locked-stage thumbnails are dark grey; a lock icon would explain them better than the word LOCKED. The tour hint card also overlaps the bottom of the arena just above the action bar.
