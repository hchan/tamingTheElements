# Online result agreement

The quick match screen shows the local result of the match. Both clients should report it to the service (RESULT frame, answered by RESULT_ACK) and compare: a disagreement would show a plain "results differ" note and offer the desync report. Also decide what the service does with two different results (no ranking yet, so only logging at first).
