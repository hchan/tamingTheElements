# Dash and roll cancels

Source of the idea: Marvel vs Capcom 2. Cancel a dash into an attack or a roll to cross an opponent; a super jump or hop. Design with the Dash rules in FUN-DESIGN 2.7; keep deterministic frame data.

## Progress

2026-11-14 (SIM_VERSION 66): dash into attack cancel from dash tick 10 and the front-loaded dash curve are done (FUN-DESIGN 2.3a, 2.7). Roll to cross an opponent and a super jump are still open.
