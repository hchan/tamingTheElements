# Break-free trinket

Source of the idea: World of Warcraft arena PvP. A once-per-fight, long-cooldown break out of any root, stun or freeze (WoW PvP trinket, about 2 minutes). Gives a defence to Snag and freeze so Snag stays fun to use. Compare with the Guard and dash-out options.
