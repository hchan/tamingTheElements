# Life totals differ wildly but bars are the same length (r15)

Fiara 679 vs Sindarosa 272, Pax 765 vs Lavax 400, yet both health bars are the same length, so a full bar means very different things and matchups are hard to read. Scale the bar to the larger total, show a handicap tag, or normalise life. See 60-phone-fight-pl.png (Life 272 vs 765).

Resolution (2026-11-14): Arcade life bars share one scale; the larger life total is a full bar, smaller totals are proportionally shorter (50% floor).
