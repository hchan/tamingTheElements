# Hero select: Fight bar hides the Earth row and there is no scroll cue

At 1280x720 the sticky Fight/Back bar covers the Earth heroes row (Pax, Rokcore, Tromnic) and the 2v2 partner card; nothing says the list scrolls, so players may think Earth is missing. The Tour pick has the same overlap (Air row dimmed behind Start tour). In 2v2 the You card clips ability text past its border ("Translucent Flame (Buff)").

Proposal: a bottom fade plus a scroll hint, or fit the picker (smaller cards, element columns) so all 12 heroes show above the bar; wrap ability text in the You card.
