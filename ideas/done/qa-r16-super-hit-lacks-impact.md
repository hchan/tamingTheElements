# Super lands as one plain number: the cut-in is great, the hit is not

After the tripling, Glacier Crash (Curomo vs Pax) deals 120 of 765 (about 16 percent), roughly 5 to 8 normal hits (normal hits are 14 to 27). The cut-in band, name and zoom are strong, but the hit itself is a normal-size white "120" with a small flash: no big burst, no extra hitstop or shake, no gold number. In SF/MvC the super hit is the biggest thing on screen. Make the super damage number larger and gold, add a one-off screen flash or stronger shake, and let the life bar drain visibly (the new shared-scale bars would show it well).
