## AI-generated icons (wired to the HUD)

Nineteen own-work SDXL icons, art-director approved 2026-11-20 (`provenance/ai-approval.json`; keyed 128 px outputs re-approved in `provenance/keyed-approval.json`): ember_bite, gale_talon, tide_surge, hurl_rock, lure_orb, then mycelium_mend (Umbrel), stinging_filament (Hyalia), stoop (Vortigal), forge_gore (Brazgor), tusk_gore (Durrok), boulder_roll (Quarrak), then kelp_lash (Nykra), molten_hide (Brazgor), whirlpool, bell_pulse (Hyalia), heal_spring (cropped and hue-shifted to teal, no ability), scorch_breath (Ouroth), wyrm_coil (Ouroth) and spore_burst (Umbrel). The installer is incremental (slots not named keep their icon) and keys light-background sources onto the dark icon backdrop (`KEYS`: radial superellipse mask, optional border-colour key, hand-traced silhouette, crop to drop floor bands, hue shift, ripple and droplet cues). `install-ai-ability-icons.py <approval.json>... [--check]` resizes them to 128 px into `public/assets/img/hud/ability-icons-ai/` (no third-party licence, see its `CREDITS.txt`); approval entries use hyphens or underscores, may carry an absolute `dir`, and entries without a slot are skipped. `src/game/hud/ability-icon-art.ts` maps ability sprites to them (emberBite, talonRake as gale_talon, hurlRock, lureOrb, myceliumMend, stingingFilament, stoop, forgeGore, tuskGore, boulderRoll, kelpLash, moltenHide, bellPulse, scorchBreath, wyrmCoil, sporeBurst); tide_surge, whirlpool and heal_spring have no ability yet; abyssal_pull (Abyssor) was REVISEd twice by the art-director (soft twin of whirlpool) and is not installed. Pinned by `tests/repo/ability-icons-ai.test.ts`. The CC-BY icons above stay unwired.
