Effect sprites in public/assets/img/fx/ are procedural and original: drawn by the scripts in this folder
(batch-a-ice.py, batch-b-fire.py, batch-c-earth.py, batch-d-air-water-chaos.py, ice-shock.py), with no third-party art.
They replace the legacy effect PNGs (still in git tag legacy-crafty-1.0). Needs Pillow and tools/assets/rig-gen/palette.py.
Run: python tools/assets/fx-gen/<script>.py [outdir, default public/assets/img/fx]

Looping projectile strips (public/assets/img/fx/loop/) and 12-frame element impact strips (public/assets/img/fx/impact/) are procedural
and original too: tools/assets/fx-gen/batch-i-loops-impacts.py with the density-field helpers in tools/assets/fx-gen/paint.py
(numpy + Pillow, plus grim_fx.py for the grade). Run: python tools/assets/fx-gen/batch-i-loops-impacts.py [outdir] [--only name,name]

original: tools/assets/fx-gen/batch-j-roster24-b1.py (same helpers). Run: python tools/assets/fx-gen/batch-j-roster24-b1.py [outdir] [--only name,name]

Real looping bodies for boulderRoll, sporeBurst, cinderSpray and stingingFilament (public/assets/img/fx/loop/*Loop.png) are cut on the CPU from AI-generated
stills made in this project and approved by the art-director (no third-party licence): tools/assets/fx-gen/batch-m-real-loops.py, pins in
tools/assets/fx-gen/provenance/real-loops.json. Run: ~/claudetmp/venv/bin/python -I tools/assets/fx-gen/batch-m-real-loops.py
Status-ring plates (freeze, daze; public/assets/img/fx/status/) are AI-generated own work, keyed and regraded by tools/assets/status-ring/ (see its README.md).
