public/assets/img/tilefloor.png (Arcade arena floor scatter tiles): original art generated procedurally by this repository's tooling
(a Python, numpy and Pillow script, WP-tile-floor, 2026-11-11, repainted in the arena-art pass, 2026-11-13, by Claude; the script is
committed as tools/assets/tile-gen/tilefloor.py and reproduces the PNG byte for byte). No pixels are copied from any other artwork.
Method: the floor itself is now the painted arenafloor.png (tools/assets/stage-gen/arena-floor.py); these tiles only scatter sparse
details over it. Columns 0-3 are transparent, columns 4-7 are small hand-placed polygon, line and ellipse details drawn at 4x,
averaged down with premultiplied alpha and feathered to nothing before the tile edge, then a 64-colour palette.
