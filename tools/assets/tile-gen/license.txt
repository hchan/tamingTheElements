public/assets/img/tilefloor.png (Arcade arena floor tiles): original art generated procedurally by this repository's tooling
(a Python, numpy and Pillow script, WP-tile-floor, 2026-11-11, by Claude; the script is committed as
tools/assets/tile-gen/tilefloor.py and reproduces the PNG byte for byte). No pixels are copied from any other artwork.
Method: per-tile blocky value noise and grain around a base colour, a bevel or seam, hand-placed polygons, lines and ellipses for the
decoration tiles, then a 64-colour palette (median cut, no dither).
