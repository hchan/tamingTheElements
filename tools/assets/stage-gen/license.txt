public/assets/img/stagebg.png (Arcade stage backdrop: skylines, crowd strips, drifters): original art generated procedurally by this
repository's tooling (a Python and Pillow script, WP-sf-stage-backdrop, 2026-11-12, by Claude; the script is committed as
tools/assets/stage-gen/stagebg.py and reproduces the PNG byte for byte). No pixels are copied from any other artwork.
Method: banded skies with blocky value noise, polygon silhouettes with a rim light, crowd silhouettes in three cheer frames, then a
128-colour palette (fast octree, no dither).
