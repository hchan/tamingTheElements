public/assets/img/stagebg.png (Arcade stage backdrop: skylines, crowd strips, drifters): original art generated procedurally by this
repository's tooling (a Python and Pillow script, WP-sf-stage-backdrop, 2026-11-12, repainted in the arena-art pass, 2026-11-13, by
Claude; the script is committed as tools/assets/stage-gen/stagebg.py and reproduces the PNG byte for byte). No pixels are copied
from any other artwork.
Method: noise-gradient skies glowing at the horizon with a faint aurora, overcast banks and motes; softened polygon silhouettes with
a painterly pass (haze, mottle, grain, light desaturation); three tiers of crowd figures drawn at 2x, lit from below by brazier pools
and rimmed by the sky, back tiers in a coloured haze, flickering flames and lit charms composited additively; three cheer frames;
kept truecolour RGBA (a small palette would band the soft light). Needs numpy + Pillow.

public/assets/img/arenafloor.png (Arcade painted arena floors, one 1920 x 536 image per stage): original art generated procedurally
by this repository's tooling (a Python, numpy and Pillow script, arena-art pass, 2026-11-13, by Claude; the script is committed as
tools/assets/stage-gen/arena-floor.py and reproduces the PNG byte for byte). No pixels are copied from any other artwork.
Method: fractal value noise and jittered Voronoi slabs built in a ground space squashed toward the wall (perspective), bump-lit
relief, per-stage detail (fire ember seams, water pools and caustics, earth moss, roots and grit, air veins and cloud), a shared
light pool, wall contact shadow and haze, grain, then one 64-colour palette per stage (median cut, Floyd-Steinberg) packed into a
256-entry PNG palette. Needs numpy + Pillow (the ramp helper is tools/assets/rig-gen/palette.py).
