Sampled effect cues in public/assets/audio/sfx_<element>_<kind>.ogg are generated by tools/audio-gen/gen.py with
MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0 (https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-SoundEffect-v2.0), a text-to-audio model.

Model licence: Apache-2.0 (stated in the model card front matter, `license: apache-2.0`; the code,
https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOSS-TTS folder moss_soundeffect_v2, is Apache-2.0 as well). The weights are public and ungated: no
account, token or click-through is needed to download them. Apache-2.0 permits use, modification and redistribution, including
commercial, and does not claim ownership of what the model produces. The model builds on Qwen3-1.7B (Apache-2.0) as its text encoder.
Nothing here depends on the model's training data being cleared beyond what its licence states; if that ever matters (a store
listing, a legal review), the 8 cues are cheap to regenerate from other sources.

Inputs: only the text prompts in tools/audio-gen/prompts.py (original wording, no reference audio). The files are post-processed
(band-limited, trimmed, faded, loudness-normalised, Ogg Vorbis encoded) by tools/audio-gen/post.py.
Run provenance (seed, steps, guidance, per-candidate metrics) is in tools/audio-gen/report.json.

Generator scripts in this folder: same licence as the repository.
