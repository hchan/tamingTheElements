# Legacy landing page (war/home.html, retired)

**Taming the Elements** by Henry Chan.

Taming the Elements is an arena-combat/strategy game about leveling and building a Garrison of elementals. In a world called
Pria, a disturbance has unfolded and it is up to you, the tamer, to restore the balance.

You begin with one elemental in your Garrison (your collection of elementals). Missions include leveling that elemental (by
gaining XP) and growing your Garrison. Capture other elementals and add them to your Garrison. Once it has grown, form a team
of 3 elementals and do battle with other elementals. Certain elementals are stronger against others (for example water does
more damage to fire) and each has different abilities (melee, range, heal, buffs), speed and health.

Inspired by Archon II, World of Warcraft Pet Battles and Marvel vs Capcom.

When ready, champion your strongest team and face the disturbance at the center of Pria.

## Features

- Melee and/or range combat
- Capturing other elementals
- Creating a team of elementals in the Battleground
- Learning the special abilities and buffs of each elemental
- Swapping to another character in battle to gain rest HP
