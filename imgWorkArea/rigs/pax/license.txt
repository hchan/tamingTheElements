Rig parts for pax: original art generated procedurally by this repository's tooling session (a Python, numpy and Pillow
scratch script written for WP-rig-pax, 2026-11-11, by Claude; the script is committed as tools/assets/rig-gen/pax.py). No pixels are copied from any other artwork.

Reference: the colours (grey-brown stone, olive-grey belly and legs, tan spikes) and the hunched, spiked silhouette were chosen by eye
to match the legacy Pax sprite (public/assets/img/map/pax.png, legacy Taming the Elements game art owned by the project owner), so
the hero stays recognisable. The legacy image itself is not used as a source of pixels.
Method: filled polygons per body part, supersampled 4x with value-noise shading, a darker rim and ridge lines, then downsampled to 1x.
SE and NE variants are the same shapes with per-view shifts and scales (head lower and larger for SE, smaller and with taller back
spikes for NE). No hand retouching.
