Rig parts for pax: original art generated procedurally by this repository's tooling session (a Python, numpy and Pillow
scratch script written for WP-rig-pax, 2026-11-11, redrawn 2026-11-12 for the richer look, by Claude; the script is committed as tools/assets/rig-gen/pax.py). No pixels are copied from any other artwork.

Reference: the hunched, spiked earth-golem silhouette follows the legacy Pax sprite (public/assets/img/map/pax.png, legacy Taming the Elements
game art owned by the project owner), so the hero stays recognisable. The legacy image itself is not used as a source of pixels.
Method: filled polygons per body part, supersampled 4x. Layered Voronoi stone plates with bevelled seams, a Lambert-lit height field and a
banded warm stone colour ramp; moss patches, vines, glowing amber cracks and crystals, bone spikes, horns and knuckle claws; a dark outline and a
light upper-left rim around every part (contrast on the blue Air floor and the sandy Earth floor); glowing amber eyes, brow, teeth. Then
downsampled to 1x. SE and NE variants are the same shapes with per-view shifts and scales (head lower and larger for SE, smaller for NE).
No hand retouching.
